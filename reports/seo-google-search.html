SEO report: Google Search & miloweiler.com

Source: Google Search Central. Written 11 Nov 2026. Branch ccr-172d8586-2aemz0.

Can a button make Milo appear in Google more often? Partly. Google has an official feature for this called Preferred sources. A visitor who clicks it tells Google they prefer miloweiler.com, so Milo shows up more often for that visitor, and only in Top Stories, AI Mode and AI Overviews.

It does not raise Milo's position in normal "blue link" results, it doesn't count as a vote, and it doesn't change what anyone else sees. Nothing a visitor can click does those things.

Done: I added a small "Add as preferred source on Google" link with the Google G icon in the footer (EN/FR/NL), and I fixed a leak where the Sanity Studio admin page could appear in Google.

Biggest real wins still to do (outside the code): Google Search Console, a Google Business Profile, and links from other sites (see §4).

1. The "preferred source" button

What Google says

From Help your audience find you as a preferred source:

  • When a user selects a site as a preferred source, its content is "more likely to appear in Top Stories" with a "preferred" badge. It can also get that badge in AI Mode and AI Overviews.
  • The feature is available globally, in every language where Google Search runs.
  • Only whole domains or subdomains are eligible. miloweiler.com qualifies; a subfolder like /blog would not.
  • Google offers three ways to add the button: a JavaScript button (publisher.js), a custom JavaScript API, or a plain deeplink: https://www.google.com/preferences/source?q=example.com.
  • The button is optional. A site can be chosen as a preferred source without it.

How much will it help Milo? Honestly, not much

Where it helpsRelevance for a photography portfolio
Top Stories (news carousel)low Top Stories mainly shows news and timely articles. Portfolio pages rarely appear there.
AI Mode / AI Overviewssome Could matter for questions like "set photographer Belgium" when an AI answer cites sources. Only for people who opted in.
Normal web results & Google Imagesnone These are the results that bring clients. The button doesn't touch them.

It's still worth having: it costs nothing, it's one small line in the footer, and Milo's fans (clients, friends, Instagram followers) are the kind of people who would click it. It's a nice extra, not a growth engine.

What I built

Add as preferred source on Google
  • Where: in the footer on every page that has one, below the language and theme switches (src/components/Footer2.jsx).
  • Labels: EN "Add as preferred source on Google", FR "Source préférée sur Google", NL "Voorkeursbron op Google". They stay on one line on mobile (tested at 390 px).
  • Why a plain link and not Google's script: publisher.js loads a script from news.google.com on every page. That costs speed, and it brings third-party cookies and requests, which the Cookie Notice would then have to cover. The deeplink opens the same Google tool in a new tab with no script on our side. If you ever want the official Google-styled button instead: 
    <script async src="https://news.google.com/swg/js/v1/publisher.js"></script>
<div google-add-preferred-source-btn data-theme="dark"></div>
  • To check before deploying: open google.com/preferences/source?q=miloweiler.com while signed in to a Google account and confirm that miloweiler.com shows up. Google says to do this first. If the site isn't listed yet, the link lands on an empty search; in that case remove the link, or wait until Google lists the site.

2. Other fix in this change

IssueFix
/studio (the Sanity admin) had the page title "Next.js", the description "Generated by Next.js" and no noindex. Google could index it as a junk result for "Milo Weiler". src/app/studio/layout.js: the title is now "Milo Weiler | Studio". src/app/layout.js: robots: noindex, nofollow (checked: <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow"> is rendered). I deliberately did not add a robots.txt Disallow, because that would stop Google from reading the noindex.
The 404 page ("Blank Film") is served by the same app-router layout, so it also had the title "Next.js". Its title is now "Page not found | Milo Weiler Photography", with noindex. It still returns HTTP 404.

4. What actually makes Milo appear more often (to-do, mostly outside the code)

  1. Google Search Console (search.google.com/search-console) is the most important step. Verify miloweiler.com (a DNS record at the domain host, or Netlify), submit https://miloweiler.com/sitemap.xml, and use URL Inspection → "Request indexing" after big new projects. It also shows which searches already bring visitors, so you can see what is working.
  2. Google Business Profile (business.google.com). For searches like "photographer Antwerp" or "fotograaf Mortsel", this is the strongest lever: a Maps listing, photos and reviews. Use exactly the same name, address and phone number as the site's structured data (Hof Savelkoul 40, 2640 Mortsel, +32 476 50 62 09).
  3. Reviews. Ask happy clients for a Google review. Reviews raise the Business Profile in local results.
  4. Links from other sites. Google uses links to discover and rank pages. Good sources: film and production credits (IMDb, production company sites), client sites ("photos: Milo Weiler" + a link), local press, photo awards, and Unsplash/Instagram/LinkedIn bios pointing to miloweiler.com.
  5. done Descriptive projects. Google calls helpful content "likely more than any other suggestion" in importance. Checked in Sanity (11 Nov 2026): all 61 projects have a description in EN, FR and NL (106–836 characters), and every photo has EN alt text. Keep doing this for each new project. FR/NL alt text is a small extra.
  6. Be patient. Google says to allow several weeks before judging any change. Re-deploying more often does not speed this up.
