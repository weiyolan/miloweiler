- **Cover:** his single best landscape shot, 1080×608 or larger.
- **Portfolio:** 20–50 photos, about 5 per category (set, brand, events, portraits, products, analog). Use JPGs of 1–5 MB with no watermarks or text overlays.

## 11. Review replies

Reply to every review, in the same language it was written in, within a few days.

- **NL:** `Bedankt [naam]! Het was een plezier om [de set / jullie event / jullie huwelijk] te fotograferen. Hopelijk tot een volgende keer!`
- **FR:** `Merci [nom] ! Ce fut un plaisir de photographier [le tournage / votre événement / votre mariage]. Au plaisir de retravailler ensemble !`
- **EN:** `Thank you [name]! It was a pleasure photographing [the set / your event / your wedding]. Hope to work together again!`

For a negative review, reply calmly, thank them, and offer to talk: `… Ik neem graag persoonlijk contact op om dit te bespreken.`
